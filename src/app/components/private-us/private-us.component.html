2. Scope of This Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to:

  • Visitors to our website www.blocknetica.io.
  • Merchants and partners who engage our payment and verification services.
  • End-users whose data may be processed during an age-verification or payment transaction.

It explains what personal data we collect, how we use it, and what rights you have.

3. What Data We Collect

We collect only the data necessary to operate our services:

a) From Website Visitors

  • Name, email address, and any information you submit through contact forms.
  • IP address, browser type, and standard web-log data (for analytics and security).

b From Merchants and Partners

  • Business name, contact person, role, business email, phone number, and billing information.
  • Technical integration data (e.g., API keys, transaction logs).

c From End-Users During Transactions or Age Verification

  • Mobile phone number (for Telco-based verification).
  • Verification response returned by the Mobile Network Operator (a simple "Over 18 – Yes/No").
  • Transaction metadata (timestamp, merchant ID, anonymised reference).
  • We do not receive or store dates of birth, ID documents, or facial images.

4. How We Use the Data

We process personal data for the following lawful purposes:

Purpose Legal Basis Description
Provide payment and age-verification services Contractual necessity To perform our services for merchants and end-users.
Verify customer age and prevent restricted sales Legal obligation / Legitimate interest To help merchants comply with the Online Safety Act 2023 and Trading Standards requirements.
Maintain security, fraud prevention, and auditing Legitimate interest / Legal obligation To detect misuse, maintain logs, and demonstrate compliance.
Communicate with merchants and partners Legitimate interest To manage business relationships and provide support.
Improve our website and services Consent / Legitimate interest To analyse usage patterns and enhance user experience.

We never sell personal data.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We share limited data only when necessary:

  • Telecom / Aggregator Partners: For Telco-based age verification (e.g., via TMT ID or directly with mobile network operators such as EE, O2, Vodafone, and Three). These partners return only a binary "Yes/No" response and do not share additional personal data.
  • Payment Processing Partners: Banks, Open Banking providers, or blockchain settlement partners required to execute payments.
  • Professional Advisors and Regulators: Legal, accounting, or compliance partners where required by law.
  • Technology Vendors: Secure cloud hosting, analytics, and monitoring tools operating under Data Processing Agreements.

All third parties are bound by GDPR-compliant contracts and act as Data Processors on our behalf.

6. Data Retention

We retain data only as long as necessary:

  • Merchant account and billing records: up to 7 years (for tax/audit).
  • Age-verification logs: normally 12 months, unless required longer for regulatory evidence.
  • Website analytics and cookies: as per our Cookie Policy.

After the retention period, data is securely deleted or anonymised.

7. Data Security

We employ industry-standard safeguards, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit (TLS) and at rest (AES-256).
  • Role-based access controls and multi-factor authentication.
  • Tamper-proof blockchain audit trails for verification events.
  • Regular security audits and penetration testing.

Despite these measures, no system is entirely secure; users transmit data at their own risk.

8. International Transfers

Where data is transferred outside the UK (e.g., to cloud providers in the EEA or other approved jurisdictions), we ensure equivalent protection via:

  • UK-approved International Data Transfer Agreements (IDTA) or
  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) adopted by the UK ICO.

9. Your Rights

Under UK-GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data.
  • Request correction or deletion ("right to be forgotten").
  • Restrict or object to processing.
  • Request data portability.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) (www.ico.org.uk).

Requests can be made to contact@blocknetica.io. We will respond within 30 days.

11. Children's Data

Our services are strictly intended for users aged 18 and over.

We do not knowingly collect or process personal data from minors.
